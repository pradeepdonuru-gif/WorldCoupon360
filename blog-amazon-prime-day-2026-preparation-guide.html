Amazon Prime Day 2026 Preparation Guide

Updated: November 2026 | By WorldCoupon360 Team | 8 min read

Amazon Prime Day is one of the biggest shopping events of the year, offering exclusive deals and discounts to Prime members. To make the most of this event, you need to be prepared. In this guide, we'll show you how to find the best deals, save money, and maximize your savings.

1. Sign up for Amazon Prime

If you're not already a Prime member, sign up for a free trial or membership to access exclusive deals and discounts.

2. Create a Wishlist

Make a list of the items you want to buy and add them to your Amazon wishlist. This will help you keep track of price drops and deal availability.

3. Use Price Tracking Tools

Use price tracking tools like CamelCamelCamel or Keepa to track price history and get notified when prices drop.

4. Check Deal Websites

Visit deal websites like WorldCoupon360 to find the best Amazon Prime Day deals and discounts.

5. Use Coupons and Discounts

Look for coupons and discounts on Amazon and apply them to your purchases to save even more.

6. Plan Your Purchases

Plan your purchases in advance and make a budget to avoid overspending.
