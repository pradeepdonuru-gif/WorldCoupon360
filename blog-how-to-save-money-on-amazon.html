How to Save Money on Amazon in 2026: 15 Working Tricks

Updated: November 2026 | By WorldCoupon360 Team | 8 min read

Amazon is the biggest online store in the world, but most people pay full price without knowing it. If you know the right tricks, you can save 20% to 60% on almost every order. In this guide, we will show you 15 tested ways to save money on Amazon in 2026.

1. Always Check Coupons on the Product Page

Before you click Buy Now, scroll down. Many products have a green or orange coupon box like "Save 10% with coupon" or "Save ₹200". You must tick the checkbox to apply it. Most shoppers miss this and pay extra. This is the easiest saving.

2. Use Lightning Deals

Go to Amazon > Today's Deals > Lightning Deals. These are limited-time deals with a timer. Discounts are often 30% to 70%. The best time to check is 12 AM, 12 PM and 8 PM when new deals go live. Add the item to cart quickly because stock finishes fast.

3. Use Subscribe & Save

If you buy the same items every month like coffee, shampoo, diapers or pet food, use Subscribe & Save. You get 5% to 15% off automatically and free delivery. You can cancel anytime after first delivery, there is no lock-in.

4. Track Price History Before Buying

Amazon prices change every day. A product that is ₹1,999 today was ₹1,299 last week. Use free price tracking tools like Keepa or CamelCamelCamel. Paste the product link and see the 6-month price graph. Buy only when price is at its lowest point.

5. Wait for Great Indian Festival and Prime Day

Never buy expensive items like phones, TVs, laptops in normal days. Wait for Great Indian Festival (October) and Prime Day (July). During these sales, bank offers + coupons + exchange offers combine and you can save up to 50%.

6. Use Bank Offers and Credit Cards

Amazon always has 10% instant discount with HDFC, ICICI, SBI and Axis cards. Check the "Offers" section on product page. Sometimes using a different card can save you ₹500 extra. Also check EMI offers if you buy above ₹3,000.

7. Check Amazon Warehouse Deals

Search for "Amazon Warehouse" or "Renewed". These are open-box or returned products checked by Amazon. You get 20% to 40% off and same return policy. Great for electronics and home items.

8. Buy Amazon Brand Products

Amazon Basics, Solimo, Symbol and Presto are Amazon's own brands. They are 30% cheaper than big brands and quality is very good for cables, bedsheets, batteries and daily items. Check ratings before buying.

9. Use Coupons from WorldCoupon360

Before checkout, visit WorldCoupon360 and search "Amazon". We list all working Amazon coupon codes, updated daily. Copy the code and paste it on Amazon checkout page in "Apply Promo Code" box. This can give you extra discount on top of sale price.

Pro Tip: Never use two coupon codes at once. Amazon allows only one promo code per order. Use the highest value one.

10. Add to Cart and Wait 24 Hours

If you don't need it urgently, add to cart and wait. Amazon sometimes sends you a "Price dropped" notification or a coupon to complete your purchase. This works best for fashion and electronics.

11. Use Free Delivery Smartly

If you don't have Prime, add items to reach ₹499 for free delivery. Don't pay ₹40 delivery for a ₹99 item. Buy 2-3 small items together. Or take Prime for 1 month during festival - it pays for itself in delivery savings.

12. Check Combo Offers

Many products have "Frequently bought together" or "Buy 2 Get 1". If you need shampoo + conditioner, buying combo is cheaper than buying separately. Always check the combo box below the product.

13. Compare Amazon vs Flipkart

Don't buy blindly. Same product can be ₹300 cheaper on Flipkart or vice versa. Open both apps and compare final price after coupon. Use our comparison guides on WorldCoupon360 to check daily.

14. Return and Re-buy If Price Drops

Amazon has 7-10 day return policy. If you bought a product and price drops within 7 days, you can return and re-buy at lower price. Keep your invoice and check price daily after purchase.

15. Use Cashback Apps

Use cashback apps and extensions before opening Amazon. You get 2% to 5% extra cashback on top of discount. It's small, but on a ₹50,000 phone, that's ₹2,500 back.

Final Checklist Before You Pay

Before you click Place Order, check this list: 1) Coupon ticked? 2) Bank offer applied? 3) Price history checked? 4) WorldCoupon360 code applied? If yes, you saved maximum money.
