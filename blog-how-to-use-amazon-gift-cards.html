How to Use Amazon Gift Cards in 2026

Updated: November 2026 | By WorldCoupon360 Team | 6 min read

Amazon gift cards are a great way to shop on Amazon without using your credit or debit card. You can buy them at a discount, use them to pay for your purchases, and even reload them. In this article, we'll show you how to use Amazon gift cards and maximize your savings.

1. Buying Amazon Gift Cards

You can buy Amazon gift cards online or in-store from various retailers. Look for discounts and promotions to save even more. You can also buy e-gift cards and have them delivered to your email.

2. Redeeming Amazon Gift Cards

To redeem an Amazon gift card, simply scratch off the activation strip and enter the claim code on Amazon's website. You can also redeem e-gift cards by clicking on the link in your email.

3. Using Amazon Gift Cards

Amazon gift cards can be used to buy anything on Amazon, including products, subscriptions, and digital content. You can even use them to pay for Amazon Prime membership.

4. Reloading Amazon Gift Cards

You can reload your Amazon gift card balance by purchasing a reloadable gift card or by using the Amazon Reload feature. This allows you to add funds to your gift card balance using cash or debit card.

5. Checking Your Balance

You can check your Amazon gift card balance by logging into your Amazon account and viewing your gift card balance. You can also check your balance on the Amazon website or mobile app.
